For anything else, including security/vulnerability disclosures, contact me at [calamityfox@purelymail.com](mailto:calamityfox@purelymail.com).

You can encrypt your message with my [**age**](misc/keys/public-key.age) or [**age**](misc/keys/public-key.gpg) public keys. I'll do my best to reply to any emails as soon as I possible. Encrypted emails *without* attached public keys will be read but I won't reply to them, since I'd have to fall back to plaintext. Plaintext emails will always be replied to, where relevant.

You can also message me on [Signal](https://signal.org). My username is [`kes.123`](https://signal.me/#eu/nM5aRSJ1kqOL0RtXQHOtgxqk6S5baJuLUNf4RxV1cu1ZIYaKd_dNBIZPvKpvRRZO).
